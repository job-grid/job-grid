Which documents may be needed for overseas employment?

Depending on the destination and job, applicants may be asked for a valid passport, CV, cover letter, National Employment Authority (NEA) clearance, police clearance, medical or health certificate, academic or professional documents for attestation, and an IELTS certificate where required. Not every applicant needs every document.

Confirm requirements before paying

Check the destination country’s immigration rules, the employer’s instructions and the relevant issuing authority. Medical examinations, language tests, clearance documents and attestations may have specific approved providers or procedures. Ask for a written breakdown of any service fee and distinguish it from government or third-party charges.
