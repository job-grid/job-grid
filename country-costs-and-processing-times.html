Source and verification status

Where official visa-category guidance is available, this page links to it and states the category; other destinations remain unquoted until a reliable source is confirmed.

What affects the real cost and timeline?

Actual costs and timeframes may depend on the specific vacancy, employer, work-permit route, visa category, medical and police checks, document requirements, appointment availability, and the completeness of an applicant’s documents. Recruitment duration and immigration processing are different stages and should be checked separately.
