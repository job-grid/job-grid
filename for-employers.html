Job Grid displays vacancies only when authorized administrators publish employer-supplied job details. Each listing should include the employer, job title, country and location, description, requirements, application instructions or official application URL, and publication/expiry details.

Publishing is controlled.Every submitted job remains private until staff review the employer and official source. Accepted submissions become unpublished drafts and require source verification and authorized publication before candidates can see them. Never send money to anyone claiming a job is guaranteed.
